HANOI RUSH Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

HANOI RUSH ("the app") does not collect information that identifies you. There are no accounts, the app never asks for your name, email, phone number, or address, and the developer runs no server.

The app does use two Google services, and information is collected in the process. One is advertising (Google AdMob); the other is usage statistics used to improve the game (Google Analytics for Firebase). This policy explains what each one collects and how you can opt out.

1. Information handled

Stored on your device only, never transmitted

The following stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

  • Game settings — theme, screen effects, sound and music volume, sound style, haptics, language, whether you have seen the tutorial, whether usage statistics are allowed
  • Game records — clear time, move count, and date for each difficulty
  • Cumulative play time and first-launch time, used to decide how often ads appear

Usage statistics collected to improve the game

To find out where players get stuck and where they give up — so the difficulty curve, the tutorial, and the screens can be fixed — the app sends the following to Google Analytics for Firebase. Turning off Settings → "Usage Stats" in the app stops this collection at any time.

DataWhat it contains

  • You are distinguished only by an app instance ID — a random value Firebase issues at installation. Deleting the app destroys it. Reinstalling issues a new one that is not linked to your earlier activity.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used for usage statistics. The analytics library the app links (FirebaseAnalyticsCore) does not include the ability to read the advertising identifier at all. IDFA is used only for the advertising purposes in section 5.
  • In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, nothing is collected until you respond to the consent form. If the app cannot determine your region — for example on a first launch with no internet connection — nothing is collected for that session either.

2. Why this information is handled

  • To keep your settings and records so you can pick up where you left off
  • To show ads and measure their performance, which covers the cost of running the app
  • To learn where players get stuck or give up, so the difficulty, the tutorial, and the screens can be improved

3. Retention and deletion

Settings and records stored on your device are deleted when you delete the app. The developer keeps no copy, so there is nothing separate to delete.

Usage statistics are retained for 14 months. Individual per-round records are deleted 14 months after the record is created, leaving only aggregated figures. Deletion runs as a monthly batch, so the actual removal can be up to a month later.

Values kept at the user level, such as your best medal tier for each difficulty, have their 14-month period restarted every time you open the app, so they persist for as long as you keep playing. Deleting the app destroys the app instance ID, so nothing further is recorded, and whatever remains is deleted once the period above passes.

Individual records are tied to the app instance ID alone, so they cannot be traced back to a particular person.

Google's retention and deletion practices are governed by Google's own policy. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising identifier and opting out

On iOS, the system asks your permission before the app can access the advertising identifier (IDFA). You may decline — you will simply see non-personalized ads instead, and nothing about the game is restricted. This choice affects advertising only; the usage statistics in section 1 do not use IDFA, so they are the same whether you allow or decline.

You can change this choice at any time.

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → HANOI RUSH
  • In regions where consent is required (the European Economic Area and others), open the app's Settings → Privacy Options to revisit your advertising consent.

6. Your rights

Because the developer holds nothing that identifies you, there is no way to single you out in order to process an access, correction, or deletion request. You control the following directly.

  • Opting out of usage statistics: turn off Settings → "Usage Stats" in the app and collection stops immediately. You can turn it back on at any time, and turning it off restricts nothing about the game.
  • Deleting usage statistics already collected: deleting the app destroys the app instance ID, which ends collection; existing records are deleted once the retention period in section 3 passes.
  • Revisiting advertising consent: in regions where consent is required, open the app's Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Settings and records on your device: deleting the app deletes them.
  • Advertising identifier: opt out using the methods in section 5.
  • Information held by Google: follow the procedures in the Google Privacy Policy.

For anything else, write to the address below and you will get a reply.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14, and it is not configured as a child-directed app with the advertising service. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children under 14.

8. Safeguards

The developer operates no server of their own and does not collect information that identifies you in the first place. Not collecting the data is the strongest safeguard this app can offer.

Settings and records on your device live in the operating system's per-app storage, which other apps cannot read. Usage statistics are transmitted over encrypted connections to systems Google operates, and the developer reviews the results only to improve the game and does not attempt to identify individual users.
