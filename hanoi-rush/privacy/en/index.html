HANOI RUSH Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

HANOI RUSH ("the app") does not collect or store your personal information directly. There are no accounts, and the developer runs no server. The app does show ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some information in the process. This policy explains what that means.

1. Information handled

Stored on your device only, never transmitted

The following stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

  • Game settings — theme, screen effects, sound and music volume, sound style, haptics, language, whether you have seen the tutorial
  • Game records — clear time, move count, and date for each difficulty
  • Cumulative play time and first-launch time, used to decide how often ads appear

Collected by Google for advertising

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following, per Google's published "App store data disclosure" (updated July 25, 2026).

DataPurpose
IP addressEstimating the approximate location of the device
Device ID (advertising identifier and similar)Third-party advertising and analytics
Advertising data (ads you have seen)Powering advertising and analytics features
Product interactions (app launch taps, video views, and similar)Improving advertising performance
Performance data (launch time, hang rate, energy usage)Understanding user behavior, planning features, displaying ads, and sharing with other entities that display ads
Crash logs not associated with a userDiagnosing problems, improving the SDK, advertising and analytics

2. Why this information is handled

  • To keep your settings and records so you can pick up where you left off
  • To show ads and measure their performance, which covers the cost of running the app

3. Retention and deletion

Settings and records stored on your device are deleted when you delete the app. The developer keeps no copy, so there is nothing separate to delete.

Google's retention and deletion practices are governed by Google's own policy. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties

The app uses the following service to show ads.

ProviderGoogle LLC (Google AdMob, User Messaging Platform)
PurposeServing ads, measuring ad performance, managing advertising consent
DataThe items listed in "Collected by Google for advertising" above
PolicyGoogle Privacy Policy

The developer shares your information with no other third party.

5. Advertising identifier and opting out

On iOS, the system asks your permission before the app can access the advertising identifier (IDFA). You may decline — you will simply see non-personalized ads instead, and nothing about the game is restricted.

You can change this choice at any time.

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → HANOI RUSH
  • In regions where consent is required (the European Economic Area and others), open the app's Settings → Privacy Options to revisit your advertising consent.

6. Your rights

Because the developer stores none of your personal information, there is nothing held by the developer for you to access, correct, or delete. You control the following directly.

  • Settings and records on your device: deleting the app deletes them.
  • Advertising identifier: opt out using the methods in section 5.
  • Information held by Google: follow the procedures in the Google Privacy Policy.

For anything else, write to the address below and you will get a reply.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14, and it is not configured as a child-directed app with the advertising service. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children under 14.

8. Safeguards

The app does not transmit or store your information on a developer-operated server. Not holding the data is the strongest safeguard this app can offer. Settings and records on your device live in the operating system's per-app storage, which other apps cannot read.
