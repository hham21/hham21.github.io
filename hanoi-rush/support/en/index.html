HANOI RUSH Support

HANOI RUSH is a mobile game where you solve the Tower of Hanoi puzzle against the clock.

Contact

Send bug reports, suggestions, and any other questions to hanoirush.help@gmail.com. You will get a reply once it is read.

When reporting a bug, please include your device model and iOS version — it makes the cause much easier to find.

Good to know

Records live on your device only

Clear times and move counts are stored on your device and never sent to a server. That means deleting the app deletes your records, and they cannot be moved to another device.

Changing your ad settings

In regions where consent is required, a Privacy Options entry appears in the app's settings screen. You can revisit your advertising consent there.

iOS tracking permission is under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking does not restrict anything in the game.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for what information the app handles.
